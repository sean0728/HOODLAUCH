Hood Launch is an independent, unofficial interface built for Robinhood Chain — it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a product of Robinhood Markets, Inc. Wallet connection is real (via your browser wallet), and the Demo/Live switch targets testnet or mainnet respectively. Once real factory addresses are configured for a network, launching a token, trading it, and its own price and chart (for a "taxed"/"graduated" token — one created through this platform's own Deploy and Add Liquidity flow) all reflect genuine on-chain transactions and live pool state, not a simulation — real tokens and funds move. What stays illustrative regardless: the holder breakdown, 24h volume, and — deliberately — the price of any pool Hood Launch merely detected rather than created itself (shown as "price isn't tracked here", since this platform doesn't vouch for those). With no factory configured, or on the fully offline demo path, everything shown is simulated and nothing real ever moves.

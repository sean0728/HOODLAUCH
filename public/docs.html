How IgnitionX works today, and how to launch on it.

IgnitionX is a token launchpad for Robinhood Chain, with pump.fun-style quick launches on Solana alongside. Every Robinhood Chain token trades on Uniswap V4. This page covers the platform as it runs now: the launch modes, how the 1% fee is taken, what happens at graduation, and a step-by-step guide to your first launch.

Jump to: Deploy a token See what's new
◆

The platform is currently running on testnet. Robinhood Chain testnet and Solana devnet use test funds with no real value. The network pill next to your wallet always shows which network is active, and the same setting applies to everyone using the site.

◆

IgnitionX is an independent, unofficial interface built for Robinhood Chain. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a product of Robinhood Markets, Inc.

01Getting started

Three things to know before you do anything else on the platform.

Connect a walletFor Robinhood Chain, click Connect Wallet and approve in whichever wallet your browser has injected (such as MetaMask). For Solana, click Connect Solana and pick a Solana wallet. There is no sign-up beyond that. You can connect either one, or both. With only a Solana wallet connected, click your name in the header to open your profile or portfolio, or to disconnect; the Robinhood button then becomes an optional extra.
Check the networkThe network pill next to your wallet shows whether the platform is on Testnet or Mainnet. It is a platform-wide setting, not a personal one. Solana devnet counts as testnet and Solana mainnet as mainnet.
Know which one is realTestnet funds have no value. Mainnet is the real chain: real ETH or SOL, real tokens, real and irreversible trades. Always check the pill before sending anything.

02Launch modes

Every launch starts the same way (name, symbol, supply), then goes one of four ways on Robinhood Chain, or the single quick-launch path on Solana.

Deploy + Add Liquidity

Needs ETH

Creates the token and pairs your ETH into a real Uniswap V4 pool in the same transaction. A 1.00% fee on trades funds the climb to a $50,000 market cap, then switches off for good. Your liquidity is locked to you.

  • CostA flat launch fee, plus the ETH you pair in
  • Fee1.00% of the ETH in each trade, until $50,000 market cap
  • OptionalAn initial buy, guaranteed to be the pool's first trade and exempt from the early-buy surcharge

Deploy + Add Liquidity (Custom Tax)

Needs ETH

The fully configurable path. Set a buy tax and a sell tax independently, and split each across reflections, marketing, automatic liquidity and automatic burn. Rates lock at launch and can never be raised. The platform's 1% fee sits on top and switches off at the same market-cap target.

  • CostA flat launch fee, plus the ETH you pair in
  • TaxYour own rates (capped per side), plus the platform fee
  • Best forReflections, auto-liquidity or fully custom tokenomics

Quick Launch

⚡ No ETH needed

No upfront liquidity. Your token trades immediately against its own bonding curve, and price rises as people buy. When the curve has raised its target, it graduates: a real Uniswap V4 pool is created automatically and trading continues there.

  • CostA flat launch fee (plus an optional first buy)
  • On the curveA 1.00% fee on the ETH of every buy and sell
  • After graduationThe pool's 1.00% fee until $50,000 market cap

Quick Launch (Custom Tax)

⚡ No ETH needed

The same bonding-curve mechanics, but the token graduates into the configurable token type, so your reflections, marketing, auto-liquidity and burn settings apply from the first pool trade.

  • CostA flat launch fee (plus an optional first buy)
  • TaxThe platform fee on the curve; your configured tax once in the pool
  • Best forA curve launch that ends in your own tokenomics

Solana Quick Launch

⚡ Solana wallet

A pump.fun-style launch on Meteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve, signed only by your Solana wallet. The token graduates to a Meteora DAMM v2 pool when the curve fills. See Solana Quick Launch for the details.

◆

Every Robinhood Chain mode that creates a pool locks the liquidity to the original creator for a fixed period. Nobody can pull it out early, and a Quick Launch's position is locked for the creator who launched it, never for whoever happens to trigger graduation. There is no deploy-only mode any more: a Quick Launch covers the case of launching without upfront liquidity.

03How to deploy a token

The launch wizard is the same four steps for every mode. Only what you fill in on steps 2 and 3 changes.

Connect your wallet and check the network

Connect the wallet for the chain you want (Robinhood Chain or Solana), confirm the network pill, then open Launch a token. On the Create page, the Robinhood / Solana picker switches the wizard to the other chain.

Step 01 · Identity

Name, ticker, total supply (where the platform lets you choose it), a short description, and optionally a logo, banner and social links. This is what appears on your token's card and page. You can still edit the description, branding and socials after launch.

Step 02 · Deploy mode

Pick one of the modes from Launch modes. The live preview on the right shows what your token's card will look like.

Not sure? No ETH to put in, or you want instant trading → Quick Launch. Want a real pool from the first second → Deploy + Add Liquidity. Want reflections, marketing or auto-liquidity → one of the Custom Tax modes.

Step 03 · Terms

For a liquidity mode, set how much ETH to pair in and, optionally, an initial buy. For a custom-tax mode, set the buy and sell split across reflections, marketing, liquidity and burn, the marketing wallet, and (once a reflection share is set) how reflections are paid. Quick Launch has almost nothing to configure here.

Step 04 · Review

Check everything. Tokenomics cannot be edited after this. Confirm, then sign: a free message signature for a gasless launch plus one ETH deposit (see Gasless launches), or an ordinary wallet transaction. A progress panel tracks each step of the launch.

You're live

Once it confirms, your token appears on the grid, a launch announcement posts to the live feed (and to the Telegram launches channel), and it is listed under Your launches in your Portfolio. See After you launch.

04Gasless launches

All four Robinhood Chain modes can be launched without paying your own gas.

1. SignYou sign a message describing your launch. It is free, uses no gas and sends no transaction.
2. DepositYou send one plain ETH transfer covering the launch fee, the liquidity and any first buy. It is an ordinary transfer, not a gas payment.
3. RelayThe platform's relayer submits the launch transaction and pays its gas. That gas is repaid out of the launch fee, up to a cap the platform sets.

You still fund the launch fee, liquidity and first buy yourself; only the transaction's gas is covered. Nobody but you can redirect or forge a launch, because the contracts re-check your signature and your deposit before doing anything. If you sign and the relayer never completes the launch, you can reclaim your deposit after the voucher's deadline. When the relayer isn't ready, the page says so and falls back to a normal wallet transaction automatically.

05Custom tax tokens

A custom-tax token lets you choose a buy tax and a sell tax independently and split each across four mechanics. Total rates are capped per side, and every rate is locked at launch with no setter afterwards.

ReflectionsA share of every trade is distributed to all holders in proportion to their balance. You choose at launch how holders are paid, and the choice is permanent (see below).
MarketingTaken in ETH from the ETH side of the trade and paid to your marketing wallet. The creator can change the wallet later, but never the rate. A wallet that can't receive ETH just keeps its share waiting until it's changed; trading is never blocked.
LiquidityTaken in the token and turned into permanent pool liquidity by a keeper. It can never be withdrawn.
BurnTaken in the token and destroyed, reducing total supply.

Choosing how reflections are paid

Same tokenHolders receive more of the token itself. Holders can claim at any time.
ETHReflection tokens are sold for ETH by a keeper and the ETH is shared out. Payouts are sent to holders automatically in batches; anyone can also claim straight from the contract. Holders owed only a dust amount keep accruing until it is worth the gas.
Another tokenThe ETH buys another token that already trades on the platform, and that token is shared out. The target is checked on-chain when you launch.
◆

A custom token pays the platform fee on top of your own rates. The platform fee switches off at the market-cap target (see Graduation); your own rates never switch off.

06Solana Quick Launch

Solana launches use Meteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve, an audited program that already runs on Solana. IgnitionX doesn't deploy a contract for it. Anyone with a Solana wallet can launch, and it currently runs on devnet with free test SOL.

LaunchYou sign two free messages and the launch transaction with your Solana wallet. Your Robinhood wallet is never asked to confirm anything. The launch can include your own first buy.
TradeAnyone buys and sells against the curve. In the grid, the token shows Bonding curve while it trades there, and its market cap is shown in dollars.
GraduateWhen the curve has raised its SOL target, Meteora migrates the liquidity to a DAMM v2 pool. Most of the migrated LP is permanently locked to the platform and a share goes to the creator. From then on the token shows as graduated.
FeesA trading fee applies on the curve; Meteora takes its protocol share first, then the creator receives a share of the remainder and the platform the rest. The numbers (starting and graduation market cap, fee, creator share) are set by the platform and fixed into each token's curve when it launches.

Solana tokens sit in the same Live launches grid, the home page's New launches window and the live feed as Robinhood Chain tokens, and use the same comments, callouts and follows. Solana buys and sells are detected from the pool and appear in the live feed within about a minute. The Robinhood Chain fee model described below (ETH-side fee, creator rewards, platform-token buyback) is specific to Robinhood Chain; Solana follows Meteora's own fee rules.

◆

Solana is on devnet. Devnet SOL is free test currency with no value. Check the network pill before launching or trading.

07After you launch

Edit your token

From your token's page (or Portfolio → Your launches), update its description, logo, banner and social links any time. A quick signature from your wallet syncs it for every visitor.

Claim creator rewards

On Robinhood Chain, 10% of the platform's 1% fee on your token's trades is credited to you in ETH, per token. Your Portfolio shows the claimable balance and you claim it straight to your wallet.

On Solana, your share of each Quick Launch token's trading fees builds up in SOL. Connect your Solana wallet and your Portfolio shows its SOL balance and holdings, plus your Solana launches, each with its unclaimed amount and a Claim creator fees button. This works with a Solana wallet alone.

Unlock your LP

When your lock period ends, an Unlock LP button appears next to that launch in your Portfolio. Until then the countdown shows how long is left.

Watch it trade

Every real buy and sell appears in the live feed, and your token's price and market-cap chart update from the same on-chain activity.

Manage a custom token

Creators of a custom-tax token can change the marketing wallet and hand the creator role to someone else (a two-step transfer), or renounce it. Rates can't be changed by anyone.

Claim reflections

If you hold a reflection token, its page shows what you're owed in the right units (the token, ETH or the payout token). Automatic batches pay most holders; you can also claim yourself.

08Buying & selling

Trades happen on the token's own page. What it trades against depends on which side of graduation the token is on.

Pool tokenDeploy + Add Liquidity and Custom Tax tokens trade in a Uniswap V4 pool from the moment they launch. Use the Buy/Sell panel on the token's page: enter an amount, see the estimate, price impact and the fees included, and confirm. Sells need one approval the first time.
Quick Launch, on the curveTrades go against the bonding curve itself. Price rises with every buy and falls with every sell. There is no pool yet and the token shows Bonding curve.
Quick Launch, graduatedIt behaves like a pool token from then on: same trade panel, same live pricing.
  • Slippage. The default is 2%. The minimum you'll accept is enforced on-chain, so a trade that would fill worse than your setting reverts instead of filling badly.
  • The estimate includes the fees. The 0.30% pool fee, the platform's 1% fee, any early-buy surcharge, and a custom token's own rates are all in the quote.
  • Live feed. Curve trades carry a small ⚡ marker so you can tell them apart from pool trades.

09How fees work

On Robinhood Chain the platform takes one 1% fee on a token's trades. It is taken in ETH, out of the trade itself, and it stops on its own once the token has grown.

Before

  • The fee was taken in the launched token.
  • To turn it into ETH, a distributor later sold those tokens in the pool.
  • Every claim created a visible sell on the chart.

Now

  • The fee comes straight off the ETH side of each buy and sell.
  • No token is sold to collect it, so nothing extra appears on the chart.
  • The ETH is paid out to its destinations in batches by the relayer.

What you pay

BuyingThe fee is added on top of the ETH that reaches the pool. If you spend 1 ETH, the pool receives the amount after the fee.
SellingThe fee is taken from the ETH you'd receive. If the pool pays out 1 ETH, you receive 1 ETH less the fee.
QuoteThe trade panel's estimate already includes it, so what you see is what you get within your slippage setting.

Where the fee goes

1% OF THE ETH IN EACH TRADETrade feeTaken from the ETH side of every buy and sell, then booked for that token's pool until the next payout.
paid out in batches by the relayer
10% OF THE FEECreator rewardsCredited to the token's creator in ETH. Claim it from your Portfolio.
90% OF THE FEEFee-wallet distributorReceives the rest as plain ETH.
the distributor splits what it receives
HALFPlatform fee walletClaimable by the platform.
HALFPlatform-token buybackBuys the platform token, then burns part and airdrops part to its holders.

Split shown is the platform's current default setting, copied into each pool when it is created; later changes apply to new launches only, never to existing pools.

The fee-wallet distributor keeps a running on-chain tally of what it received, paid to the fee wallet and spent on platform-token buybacks, so these flows can be checked against the contract rather than taken on trust.

The other charges

Launch feeA flat fee at launch. When the launch is relayed, the relayer's gas is repaid from it first and the rest is split between the platform treasury and the platform rewards.
Curve feeA Quick Launch charges 1% of the ETH on every curve buy and sell until the curve graduates, split the same way as above.
Custom ratesA custom-tax token's own reflection, marketing, liquidity and burn rates, set by the creator. Marketing is taken in ETH; the others in the token.
Early-buy surchargeAn extra charge on buys in a new pool's first minutes. See below.
Pool feeThe 0.30% Uniswap V4 pool fee, which goes to the pool's liquidity providers.

10Graduation & anti-snipe

Every token is built to stop being charged the platform fee once it has grown, and new pools protect early buyers from bots.

The fee switches itself off

  • A pool token starts with the 1% platform fee the moment it launches. The fee permanently switches off once the pool's market cap has stayed at or above the target ($50,000 by default) across two checks at least 30 minutes apart. Two checks stop a single price spike from ending it early.
  • A Quick Launch charges its curve fee until the curve graduates, then the pool's 1% fee runs until the same $50,000 target is reached and switches off.
  • The market cap is measured in dollars from the pool price and a live ETH price feed. If the feed is stale or unavailable the fee simply stays on and trading is never blocked.
  • What switches off is the platform fee. A custom-tax token's own rates, including its marketing share, keep working.

On the grid, a token shows Graduated once its fee has switched off. A curve token first shows Bonding curve, and shows as live on a DEX only once it has moved into a pool.

Early-buy surcharge

A new pool charges an extra fee on buys only that starts high at the moment the pool is created and falls in a straight line to zero over a short window. A bot buying in the first blocks hands much of its position to the platform; someone buying a couple of minutes later pays little or nothing. Sells are never charged.

  • The buy quote shows it as "includes X% early-buy surcharge, falling to 0", so you know what you're paying before you confirm.
  • It is taken in ETH and goes to the platform fee side.
  • Each pool keeps the surcharge settings it was created with, so nobody can raise it on a pool that is already trading.
  • The creator's own first buy inside the launch is exempt. For a Quick Launch the window starts when the pool is created at graduation, not on the curve.
◆

Nobody can raise a rate after launch. A custom token's rates have no setter at all, and a platform fee only ever moves one way: from on to permanently off.

Telegram posts

New launches, and the two milestones (a curve graduating to a pool, and a token's fee switching off) are announced in the platform's Telegram launches channel, once each.

12Security & disclosures

Locked liquidity

Every Robinhood Chain launch locks its liquidity position to the creator for a fixed period. Neither the creator, the platform nor anyone else can pull it out early.

Immutable custom rates

A custom-tax token's rates are fixed at launch. No creator, admin or platform action can raise them. Only the marketing wallet can change, and only the creator can change it.

Payouts never block trading

Fees are booked during a trade and paid out separately. A destination that can't receive ETH has its amount held and retried; it can never make a buy or sell fail.

Your keys stay yours

The relayer only holds a wallet used to pay gas. It can't take your funds or forge a launch, because every launch is checked against your own signature and deposit.

Independently-added pools

If liquidity appears for a token outside IgnitionX's own launch flow, its card says so and its price isn't tracked here. The platform doesn't vouch for a market it didn't create.

Testnet vs. mainnet

The network switch is platform-wide. On mainnet every launch, trade and price reflects real on-chain activity and real funds.

13FAQ

Do I need ETH to launch a token?

Only for the modes that add liquidity upfront (Deploy + Add Liquidity and its Custom Tax version), where you pair that ETH into the pool yourself. The Quick Launch modes need no upfront liquidity, only the flat launch fee and an optional first buy.

Why doesn't claiming rewards show a sell on the chart any more?

The platform's 1% fee is now taken in ETH directly from each trade, so there are no fee tokens to sell. Rewards are paid out in ETH from what was already collected.

Who pays the fee on a trade?

The trader. A buyer pays it on top of the ETH that reaches the pool, and a seller receives that much less ETH. The trade panel's estimate already includes it.

When does the platform fee stop?

When the token's market cap has stayed at or above the target ($50,000 by default) across two checks at least 30 minutes apart. After that the platform fee is off for good. A custom token's own rates continue.

Can a creator raise the tax after launch?

No. A custom token's rates are locked at launch with no setter anywhere in the contract. The platform fee only ever goes from on to off.

What does "graduated" mean?

Two things, depending on the token. For a pool token it means the platform fee has permanently switched off. For a Quick Launch it also marks the moment the token left the bonding curve for a real pool; until then it's labelled Bonding curve. On Solana, graduating means the curve filled and the liquidity moved to a Meteora DAMM v2 pool.

What happens to my liquidity?

It is locked to you, the creator, for a fixed period as soon as it's added. You can't withdraw it early and neither can anyone else. When the lock ends, an Unlock LP button appears in your Portfolio.

Can I launch from Solana and from Robinhood Chain with one account?

Yes. Either wallet can be your account's main wallet, and you can link the other so your name, picture, follows and launches follow you across both. See Profiles & social.

What's the difference between Testnet and Mainnet?

Testnet runs on Robinhood Chain's test network and Solana devnet, where nothing has real value. Mainnet is the real chain, with real and irreversible trades. The network pill next to your wallet shows which is active, and it is the same for everyone.

Is IgnitionX affiliated with Robinhood?

No. IgnitionX is an independent, unofficial interface built for Robinhood Chain. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a product of Robinhood Markets, Inc.
